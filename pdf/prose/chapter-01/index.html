Indian Civilization and Culture – Class 12 English

Author: Mahatma Gandhi

This chapter discusses the meaning of true civilization, moral duty, self-restraint and the difference between material progress and spiritual development. It is an important prose chapter for Bihar Board Class 12 English preparation.

Chapter Summary

Mahatma Gandhi explains that civilization is not merely the growth of physical comforts, machines or material possessions. True civilization teaches people to perform their duties, control their desires and live according to moral values. The chapter emphasizes self-discipline, simplicity and spiritual development. Gandhi contrasts these ideas with excessive materialism and argues that real progress should improve human character as well as living conditions.

Important Topics

  • Meaning and definition of civilization
  • Indian civilization and moral values
  • Self-restraint and duty
  • Material progress versus spiritual development
  • Importance of simplicity and ethical living

Important Questions

  2. What is the main idea of Indian Civilization and Culture?
