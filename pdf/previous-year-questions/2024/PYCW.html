Complete Coverage of Every Single Sub-part (a to j)

1. Write an essay on any one of the following in about 150-200 words:

a) Value of Education
Education is the most powerful weapon to change society and foster individual growth. It empowers minds with critical thinking, ethical values, and practical skills necessary for career development. Quality education removes social inequalities, eradicates poverty, and drives economic progress. Beyond acquiring degrees, true education instills discipline, empathy, and civic responsibility, making individuals valuable contributors to national development.
b) Chandrayaan 3
Chandrayaan 3 is a landmark achievement in India's space exploration history, executed by ISRO. With the successful soft landing of the Vikram lander on the unexplored lunar South Pole, India became the fourth nation to land on the Moon and the first at the southern pole. This mission proved India's cost-effective technological excellence, advancing scientific research regarding lunar water ice, mineral composition, and future deep-space exploration.
c) An Indian Festival
Indian festivals reflect the rich cultural heritage, unity, and joyful spirit of our nation. Festivals like Diwali, Chhath Puja, and Eid bring families and communities together across religious boundaries. Homes are decorated with lamps, traditional sweets are shared, and cultural rituals are performed. These celebrations foster harmony, spread happiness, and remind us of the triumph of righteousness and light over darkness.
d) Wonders of Science
Science has revolutionized every aspect of human life, making daily tasks easier and expanding human potential. From modern transport and instant global communication to life-saving medical treatments, scientific discoveries have reshaped the world. Electricity, space research, and computer technology stand out as monumental marvels. However, scientific progress must be guided by human moral values to prevent environmental harm and destruction.
e) Noise Pollution
Noise pollution is a growing environmental hazard caused by industrial machinery, vehicular traffic, loudspeakers, and construction activities. Continuous exposure to high decibel levels leads to stress, hearing impairment, sleep disturbances, and cardiovascular issues. Strict enforcement of noise regulations, planting green belts, and raising public awareness about quiet zones are essential steps to combat this issue.

2. Explain any one of the following lines (Prose):

a) "A certain degree of physical harmony and comfort is necessary, but above a certain level it becomes a hindrance instead of help."
Answer: Taken from Mahatma Gandhi's essay Indian Civilization and Culture. Gandhi Ji explains that basic physical needs must be met for human survival, but unchecked indulgence in luxury and material comfort enslaves moral character and distracts people from spiritual self-realization.
b) "Fancy a lad spending his time playing around like that with gourds! ..."
Answer: Taken from Shiga Naoya's story The Artist. This line is spoken by Seibei's ethics teacher, who scolds Seibei for polishing a gourd during class, showing how adults often fail to recognize and appreciate artistic passion in children.
c) "In non-technocratic societies, except for remarkable accidents, birth is always attended."
Answer: Taken from Germaine Greer's essay A Child is Born. The author emphasizes that in traditional societies, childbirth is a collective, supportive family affair, unlike the isolated, clinical environment of modern Western hospitals.
d) "My life has been too crowded with travels and many people for me to put it all within the covers of one book, ..."
Answer: Taken from Pearl S. Buck's travelogue India Through a Traveller's Eye. The author reflects on her rich global experiences, noting that her deep connection with diverse people makes it impossible to capture every detail in a single work.

3. Explain any one of the following lines (Poetry):

a) "He hath no desire, nor sense, / Nor halfe so short a way; / Then feare not mee,"
Answer: Taken from John Donne's poem Sweetest Love, I Do Not Goe. The poet reassures his beloved that his journey will be faster and more certain than the sun's, as he possesses strong passion, desire, and emotional affection to guide him back.
b) "Cold, impossible, ahead / Lifts the mountain's lovely head."
Answer: Taken from W.H. Auden's poem Now the Leaves Are Falling Fast[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span). The line uses the image of distant, majestic cold mountain peaks to symbolize ultimate peace and liberation after the trials and isolation of human existence.
c) "You may meet him in a by-street, you may see him in the square / But when a crime's discovered, then Macavity's not there!"
Answer: Taken from T.S. Eliot's poem Macavity: The Mystery Cat[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). The poet humorously highlights Macavity's masterly criminal skill—he can be seen anywhere in public, yet vanishes without leaving a trace whenever a crime occurs.
d) "And yet those voices: / If you were not afraid, you would kill him!"
Answer: Taken from D.H. Lawrence's poem Snake[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). The poet depicts his inner psychological conflict: while he personally admires the majestic snake, his societal education urges him to destroy it out of fear.

5. Answer any five of the following in about 40-50 words each:

a) What does Pearl S. Buck mean by saying 'Religion is ever present in Indian life'?
Answer: Pearl S. Buck means that spiritual values, faith, and moral consciousness naturally influence the daily conduct, hospitality, and life decisions of people in India across all social levels[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
b) What did the doctor advise Benjy's parents to ensure his mental growth?
Answer: The doctor advised Benjy's parents to give him a simple practical task like keeping hens, which would occupy his mind, teach him responsibility, and support his growth[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
c) What do you mean by the term 'free press'?
Answer: 'Free press' refers to an independent media free from government control, censorship, or commercial suppression, allowing accurate reporting of truth and public commentary[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
d) How are the children of the joint family in Bangladesh looked after?
Answer: In traditional joint families in Bangladesh, children are raised collectively by aunts, grandmothers, and female relatives, sharing warmth and care rather than relying solely on the biological mother[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
e) Is the speaker in 'The Soldier' afraid of death?
Answer: No, the speaker in 'The Soldier' is not afraid of death[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span). He feels deeply patriotic and believes that if he dies fighting on foreign soil, that patch of land will become eternally English[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
f) Why did the speaker like the snake?
Answer: The speaker liked the snake because it appeared harmless, quiet, and majestic, quenching its thirst peacefully at his water trough like a quiet guest in nature[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
g) How are autumn and summer related to spring?
Answer: Summer matures the seeds and flowers initialized in spring, while autumn brings those fruits to full maturity, completing the seasonal life cycle[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
h) Name three areas of English language in which rapid changes have been noticed in the last few decades.
Answer: Rapid changes in English over recent decades are observed in vocabulary (due to digital tech), global pronunciation/accents, and informal digital grammar/spelling norms[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).
i) What has made English the most gloriously, empire language?
Answer: English became a dominant world language due to the historical expansion of the British Empire, economic dominance, and its flexibility in borrowing foreign words[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).
j) What did the 18th century grammarians share with the Age?
Answer: The 18th-century grammarians shared the era's passion for order, standardization, and refining rules to make English clear, precise, and permanent[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).
