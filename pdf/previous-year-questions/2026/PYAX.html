1. Write an essay on any one of the following (150-200 words):

(i) The Future of Artificial Intelligence
AI will transform healthcare, education, and transport using smart machines. However, it poses risks like job loss and cyber threats. Safe regulation is necessary for its proper use.
(ii) The Importance of Discipline in Life
Discipline ensures success, self-control, and proper time management. It builds good character in students and maintains peace and order in society.
(iii) Role of Renewable Energy in Combating Climate Change
Renewable energy sources like solar and wind power reduce carbon emissions and global warming. Shifting from fossil fuels protects our environment for future generations.
(iv) Deforestation
Deforestation means cutting down trees on a large scale. It causes soil erosion, disrupts rainfall, and destroys wildlife habitats. Plantations must be promoted to stop it.
(v) Floods: Causes and Impacts
Floods are caused by heavy rainfall and poor drainage. They damage crops, destroy homes, and cause disease outbreaks. Better water management can reduce their impact.
