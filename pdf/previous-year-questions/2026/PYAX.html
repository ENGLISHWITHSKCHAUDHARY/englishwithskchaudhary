6. Answer any three of the following in about 100-120 words:

Q6 (a) Write the summary of any one of the following poems:
(i) Fire-Hymn (ii) Now the Leaves are Falling Fast (iii) The Soldier
(i) Fire-Hymn: Written by Keki N. Daruwalla, the poem describes a burning ghat where the poet sees half-burnt corpses. As a Parsi, he regrets consigning his firstborn to fire instead of the Tower of Silence and vows to save fire from the sin of forgetfulness.

(ii) Now the Leaves are Falling Fast: Written by W.H. Auden, it highlights human frustration and loneliness. Just as falling leaves signify approaching winter, human aging leads toward death, though nature's beauty offers hope.

(iii) The Soldier: Written by Rupert Brooke, it is a patriotic sonnet where a soldier expresses deep love for England, wishing that if he dies abroad, that foreign land will forever belong to his homeland.
Q6 (b) Write the summary of any one of the following prose pieces:
(i) The Earth (ii) Indian Civilization and Culture (iii) A Pinch of Snuff
(i) The Earth: Written by H.E. Bates, it tells the story of Benjy, a simple boy who starts keeping hens. He eventually buys land, marries Florence, and drives his innocent parents out of their home.

(ii) Indian Civilization and Culture: Written by Mahatma Gandhi, it highlights that Indian civilization emphasizes moral duty, spiritual growth, and self-restraint, unlike Western civilization which focuses on materialism.

(iii) A Pinch of Snuff: Written by Manohar Malgaonkar, it is a funny story about Nanukaka who uses trickery, wit, and grand appearances to meet a welfare minister in Delhi without a prior appointment.
Q6 (c) Write a short note on any one of the following:
(i) Dramatic Structure (ii) Types of Drama (iii) African Novel in English
(i) Dramatic Structure: It includes Exposition (Introduction), Rising Action, Climax, Falling Action, and Denouement (Resolution) that shape a play's story.

(ii) Types of Drama: Major forms include Tragedy (sad ending), Comedy (happy ending), Tragicomedy (mixed elements), Farce (exaggerated humor), and Melodrama.

(iii) African Novel in English: African English literature reflects post-colonial struggles, cultural identity, and social changes, made popular worldwide by authors like Chinua Achebe.

7. Read the passage carefully and answer the questions that follow OR Write a Precis:

Q7 (Passage). Read the following passage and answer the questions:
Passage: Social media has revolutionized how we communicate, share information, and connect with people globally. Platforms like Instagram, Facebook, and X allow users to share thoughts, photos, and videos instantly. Social media inspires creativity, spreads awareness, and builds strong online communities. However, excessive use can lead to distractions, unhealthy comparisons, and mental health issues. Moreover, the spread of fake news and negativity remains a major drawback. Using social media responsibly is essential to enjoy its benefits while avoiding harm.
Questions & Short Answers:

(i) How does social media help people stay connected?
Answer: Social media allows people to share thoughts, photos, and videos instantly across the world.

(ii) What are the positive uses of social media?
Answer: It inspires creativity, spreads awareness, builds online communities, and keeps people connected.

(iii) What problems can social media cause?
Answer: Excessive use causes distraction, mental health issues, unhealthy comparisons, and fake news.

(iv) Which word in the passage means 'imagination'?
Answer: Creativity
OR Q7 (Precis). Write a precis of the following passage and give a suitable title:
Original Paragraph for Precis: Sustainable development aims to meet the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs. It balances economic growth, environmental protection, and social well-being. By adopting renewable energy, conserving natural resources, and reducing pollution, we can ensure a healthier planet for the future.
Title: Sustainable Development

Precis Answer: Sustainable development balances economic growth with environmental protection. Promoting renewable energy, conserving resources, and reducing pollution help satisfy present requirements while protecting the planet for future generations.
