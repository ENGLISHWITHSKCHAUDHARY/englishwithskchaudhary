Q1. Essays (150 - 200 Words)

(i) The Future of Artificial Intelligence
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the world rapidly. In the future, AI will play a central role in healthcare, education, transportation, and agriculture. Smart machines will help doctors diagnose diseases accurately, assist teachers in personalized learning, and drive self-driving vehicles. However, AI also brings challenges like job displacement, cyber threats, and loss of human touch. To maximize its benefits, humans must control technology with ethical rules and proper safety standards.
(ii) The Importance of Discipline in Life
Discipline is the key to success in every sphere of life. It means following rules, respecting time, and maintaining self-control. For students, discipline in study schedules leads to academic excellence. In daily life, it promotes good health, mental peace, and proper time management. Without discipline, society falls into chaos. Therefore, developing discipline early in life helps build character and ensures a bright future.
