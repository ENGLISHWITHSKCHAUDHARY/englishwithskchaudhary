1. Write an essay on any one of the following (150-200 words):

(a) Discipline
Discipline means following rules, regulations, and maintaining an ordered life. It is essential for personal growth, academic achievement, and social harmony. A disciplined person manages time effectively, builds strong moral character, and achieves goals with consistency. Without discipline, chaos prevails in both individual lives and society.
(b) My Happiest Day in School
My happiest day in school was the day our school celebrated its Annual Sports Day. I participated in the 100-meter race and secured the first position. Receiving the trophy from the principal amidst applause from friends and teachers filled me with immense joy and unforgettable memories.
(c) Noise Pollution
Noise pollution refers to excessive, undesirable sound in the environment caused by vehicles, loudspeakers, factories, and construction sites. It severely harms human health, leading to hearing loss, stress, sleep disturbances, and high blood pressure. Strict noise regulations and public awareness are necessary to curb it.
(d) Many hands make work light
This proverb highlights the power of teamwork and cooperation. When a heavy or complex task is divided among many individuals, it becomes easier, faster, and less burdensome to complete. Unity and collective effort always lead to success in any group activity or community work.
(e) My Aim in Life
Every person must have a definite aim in life to guide their actions. My aim in life is to become a teacher. Teaching allows one to educate young minds, spread knowledge, and build a strong nation. I work hard every day to fulfill this dream and serve society.
